WHAT THE SIGMA COLUMN IS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
days with exactly one contributing station: 56288
   of those, sigma != 0 on 54321 (96.5%)
   -> sigma cannot be a plain dispersion across stations on those days;
      before 1981 the count column is fixed at 1 by convention.
first day with more than one station: 1981-01-01
monthly sigma reproduced as sqrt(SUM(N_d sigma_d^2)/SUM(N_d)): n=2502, max|diff|=0.0500, within rounding for 100.00%
   -> the published monthly sigma is a pooled station dispersion,
      NOT a standard error of the monthly mean.
